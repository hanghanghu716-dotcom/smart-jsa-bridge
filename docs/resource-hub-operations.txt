Safety resource hub — operations and release checks (2026-10-28)

Public entry points: /:locale/search and /:locale/resources across the 19 supported routes.
Unified search uses public JSA/Case Study metadata, guides and approved resources.
Existing saved content, PDFs and authoring workflows are unchanged.
Topic follows are private, in-site subscriptions; they do not send email.
These directory/search pages intentionally use noindex,follow with readable initial HTML.

Operator workflow
Open /ko/admin/resources from Community. Existing administrator verification and MFA apply.
A source must have a reviewed policy before enabling collection or expanded reuse.
Public access is not a republication licence. Official sites and RSS feeds may have exclusions.
For summaries/translations, confirm commercial and adaptation rights, item-specific evidence,
third-party exceptions, jurisdiction, accuracy and privacy, and provide attribution.
Keep uncertain material pending. Linking to an official original does not authorize copying it.
Publishing is an explicit operator action; collection never publishes an item.
Use withdrawal or source suspension for immediate removal. Suspending requires no new evidence.
Policy changes send affected published entries back for review. Reviews expire after 90 days.
Review inboxes have 100-item pages. Audit records retain operator actions.
No copyrighted full articles, images or PDFs are imported by this feature.

Do not describe configured or failed sources as successfully collected, or claim worldwide
copyright clearance. Robots permission is not a copyright licence.

Except HSE's licensed title metadata, new collection retains URLs and available dates only.
The source name plus a neutral placeholder is generated locally. No article headings,
descriptions, bodies, images, PDFs or translations are retained by link-only collection.
The operator must replace the placeholder with an original descriptive label and complete
rights / accuracy / privacy / jurisdiction checks. DB triggers reject unchanged placeholders.
The inbox shows original URL paths to distinguish items and links to collection evidence;
collection evidence is not automatically accepted as commercial republication permission.
Source commercial/adaptation flags remain false until explicitly reviewed by an operator.

The collector uses fixed HTTPS hosts and paths, bounded responses (1 MiB, 40 entries/source),
robots checks, no redirects, concurrency 4, a bounded run, a 20-hour lease and a private Vault
credential with service-only RPC. Discovered PDFs are links only and are never downloaded.
Server-generated provenance cannot be removed through the review form. Repeated collection
does not overwrite reviewed or withdrawn entries. Insert counts exclude existing URLs.
Cron safety-resource-metadata remains active daily at 06:15 UTC (15:15 Korea).
Collection permission review expires 2027-01-26; changing the ordinary source review does
not silently renew the separately reviewed collection endpoints or permission window.
No paid translation/crawling service was added; existing Supabase quotas still apply.

Feedback is separate from the resource queue: /ko/admin/requests, reachable from Community.
The chat-bubble widget is FeedbackWidget / FeedbackForm. It stores submitted requests,
not a general archive of every unsubmitted conversation or keystroke.
