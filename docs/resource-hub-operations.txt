Safety resource hub — operations and release checks (2026-10-28)

Public entry points: /:locale/search and /:locale/resources across the 19 supported routes.
Unified search uses public JSA/Case Study metadata, guides and approved resources.
Existing saved content, PDFs and authoring workflows are unchanged.
Topic follows are private, in-site subscriptions; they do not send email.
These directory/search pages intentionally use noindex,follow with readable initial HTML.

Operator workflow
Open /ko/admin/resources from Community. Existing administrator verification and MFA apply.
A source must have a reviewed policy before enabling collection or expanded reuse.
Public access is not a republication licence. Official sites and RSS feeds may have exclusions.
For summaries/translations, confirm commercial and adaptation rights, item-specific evidence,
third-party exceptions, jurisdiction, accuracy and privacy, and provide attribution.
Keep uncertain material pending. Linking to an official original does not authorize copying it.
Publishing is an explicit operator action; collection never publishes an item.
Use withdrawal or source suspension for immediate removal. Suspending requires no new evidence.
Policy changes send affected published entries back for review. Reviews expire after 90 days.
Review inboxes have 100-item pages. Audit records retain operator actions.
No copyrighted full articles, images or PDFs are imported by this feature.

Country coverage and collection limits
19 official navigation entries are available; country/province and UI language remain distinct.
Source commercial/adaptation permissions start false. Unverified sources remain link-only.
HSE RSS successfully produced 10 private pending entries in the deployment verification.
Safe Work Australia RSS timed out; use official links/manual registration until it is reachable.
Other countries currently use the official directory and manual operator review, not automatic feeds.
Do not describe all countries as automatically collected or legally cleared for republication.
The collector uses fixed HTTPS hosts, bounded responses, no redirects, a 20-hour lease,
a private Vault credential and a service-only RPC. No admin secrets are added to the frontend.
Cron safety-resource-metadata runs daily at 06:15 UTC (15:15 Korea) when enabled.
It was paused for the user-requested pause and must be enabled only after the web release succeeds.
