# 3번 내용 검수 — 14차 기록 (2026-10-16)

이 문서는 14차 검수 당시 기록입니다. 최신 결과는 [15차 검수 기록](recommendation-content-review-batch15-20261016.md)을 참고하십시오.

**누적 진행: 53/180 → 72/180개 검토.** 캐나다 공통·앨버타·브리티시컬럼비아 초안 9개를 교정하고 기술 9건, 번역·용어 9건, 브리티시컬럼비아 정비 지역 검수 1건을 추가했습니다. 이번 지역 부분 검토 8건은 완료 수에 포함하지 않았습니다.

완료는 정확한 내용·출처 해시에 대응하는 AI 문서 검수 기록이 있다는 의미입니다. 독립 전문가·원어민 인증이나 현장 준법 확인, 배포 승인이 아닙니다. 이번 번역 영역은 영어 원문과 영어 초안의 의미·용어 검토입니다. 기존 53건의 검수는 그대로 유효하며, 출처 연결은 57/57개입니다.

## 수정 내용

- 정비·필터: 개인 잠금과 작업자별 검증을 명시했습니다. 개인 잠금 방식과 그룹 잠금 방식의 차이를 유지했습니다.
- 브리티시컬럼비아 정비: 적용 관할을 조건으로 추가하고, 열쇠 관리·그룹 절차·조합식 잠금 금지·예외 범위를 명확히 했습니다.
- 용접: 환기로 노출을 충분히 통제하지 못할 때의 호흡 보호를 명확히 하고, 도장·도금 제외 대상이 모재임을 분명히 했습니다.
- 앨버타 용접: 환기 고장 즉시 경고, 보호 조치와 사용자 교육 확인을 추가했습니다.
- 브리티시컬럼비아 용접: 제거·대체의 우선 검토와 노출관리계획의 연례 검토를 추가했습니다.

## 지역 검수 범위

브리티시컬럼비아 정비는 WorkSafeBC Part 2와 Part 10의 적용 범위, 정의, 개인·그룹 잠금, 작업자 검증 및 예외 조항을 대조했습니다. 개별 사업장이나 완전한 그룹 작업 절차를 승인한 것은 아닙니다.

앨버타는 2023년 기준 고지가 있는 검색 사이트에 더해, 정부가 연결한 2025년 시행 King's Printer 공식 PDF의 Part 15를 확인했습니다. 핵심 에너지 차단 조항은 대조했지만, 적용 범위·후속 개정과 필터의 배관 분류 검토가 남아 있습니다. 용접의 Part 26을 새 PDF와 대조했다고 계산하지 않았습니다.

## 초안별 결과

### 캐나다 공통 · 기계 정비

`regional-ca-maintenance`

**기술:** The revision now includes stopping, personal lockout and restraint of movable parts. The verification hold still prevents entry until isolation and stored-energy control are established.

**번역·용어:** Lockout is a physical securing measure, not merely shutdown; release or restrain preserves the appropriate response to the energy. Required conditions do not claim that a check has already been performed.

**지역 미완료 사유:** CCOHS is national technical guidance. A country-level card cannot establish which federal, provincial or territorial requirements govern a workplace; the regime-specific comparison remains incomplete.

### 앨버타 · 기계 정비

`regional-ca-ab-maintenance`

**기술:** Stopping, energy isolation, personal securing and each worker's test/confirmation are retained as separate steps. The wording refers to the applicable individual or group procedure rather than treating one operator's check as sufficient for all workers.

**번역·용어:** Identified personal locks and each worker retain distinct actors. Inoperative or otherwise made safe in the verification text is not used to authorize the excluded live-work case.

**지역 미완료 사유:** The 2025 King's Printer Part 15 text was compared with the older search-site capture. The core 212-215 duties agree in substance. Provincial coverage, relevant definitions and any later amendments still need reconciliation before regional closure.

### 브리티시컬럼비아 · 기계 정비

`regional-ca-bc-maintenance`

**기술:** Parts must be secured against movement as well as energy isolated. Personal locks/key control and the alternative location of personal locks in a compliant group system are explicit. Each worker still verifies isolation before entry.

**번역·용어:** Energy-isolating device is distinct from a control switch. Personal key control does not import the CCOHS no-master-key wording into the BC definition. Group locking does not remove each worker's verification duty.

**지역:** Compared Parts 2.1 and 10.1-10.12 with this bounded isolation card. ### 캐나다 공통 · 압축공기 필터

`regional-ca-pressure-filter`

**기술:** Personal lockout is now explicit. The inlet, outlet, bypass and drain-path check precedes safe pressure relief; the prohibition on loosening a housing to test pressure remains. No Parker valve order or torque is generalized.

**번역·용어:** Authorized and depressurization now follow consistent Canadian English. Supply, backfeed and trapped air remain distinct; the non-air and breathing-air exclusions are unchanged.

**지역 미완료 사유:** No individual Canadian workplace regime or pressure-equipment classification is established by CCOHS, OSHA or the Parker example; those applicability checks remain incomplete.

### 앨버타 · 압축공기 필터

`regional-ca-ab-pressure-filter`

**기술:** The revised text preserves each worker's test and confirmation alongside personal securing. Continued checks address pressure return. Piping is explicitly separate, so machinery isolation is not presented as a substitute for piping provisions.

**번역·용어:** Individual versus group securing is preserved without weakening each worker's verification. Depressurized describes the verified state; closed valves alone do not assert that state.

**지역 미완료 사유:** The 2025 official Part 15 text confirms separate piping provisions at 215.4-215.5. Equipment-versus-piping classification and the applicable pressure-equipment regime remain unresolved; the newly found edition does not resolve those questions.

### 브리티시컬럼비아 · 압축공기 필터

`regional-ca-bc-pressure-filter`

**기술:** Personal lockout and each worker's verification are reinforced; the group procedure remains conditional. The housing stays closed when safe pressure relief or the boundary is uncertain.

**번역·용어:** Personal lockout arranged is an applicability condition, not evidence of completion. Pressure return remains conditional, while the no-loosening prohibition remains unconditional.

**지역 미완료 사유:** BC Part 10 supports the energy-control elements, but the particular filter classification and separate pressure-equipment requirements have not been reconciled.

### 캐나다 공통 · 용접 흄

`regional-ca-welding-fume`

**기술:** Ventilation insufficiency now explicitly requires suitable respiratory protection. Hood positioning, performance rechecks and the stop condition remain; the card does not prescribe a universal air-speed value.

**번역·용어:** Source extraction is clarified as local exhaust ventilation. Coated material now means base metal rather than ambiguously excluding coated electrodes; identified consumables remain required.

**지역 미완료 사유:** National guidance and US comparison do not establish the applicable Canadian exposure limits, welding-standard edition or respiratory programme duties.

### 앨버타 · 용접 흄

`regional-ca-ab-welding-fume`

**기술:** The ventilation-failure warning, worker training and protection arrangements from the captured Part 26 are added to verification. Specified performance and stop-on-failure remain, without inventing a design flow rate.

**번역·용어:** Immediate failure warning differs from a routine pre-use check. Make-up air, discharge and maintenance remain separate from hood positioning; authorized is consistent across the labels.

**지역 미완료 사유:** Part 26 is a general ventilation provision. Welding-specific standards, exposure limits and respiratory requirements remain unreviewed. The official 2025 PDF was located but its Part 26 was not compared in this batch.

### 브리티시컬럼비아 · 용접 흄

`regional-ca-bc-welding-fume`

**기술:** The correction restores elimination/substitution before engineering controls and connects respiratory protection to residual exposure. It adds the advisory's annual exposure-plan review without treating a working fan as proof of adequate protection.

**번역·용어:** The exposure plan covers nearby workers as well as welders. Coated base metal is distinguished from consumables, and annual plan review is distinguished from checks after hood movement.

**지역 미완료 사유:** The official page for 12.112 and 12.114 shows a referenced welding standard and fixed-station local-exhaust duty. The incorporated standard, designated-substance/exposure provisions and respiratory programme have not been fully reconciled; regional completion remains pending.
