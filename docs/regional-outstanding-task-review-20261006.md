## Russia: primary originals and amendments

The Ministry of Justice publication service hosts original scans rather than relying on an undated training presentation. Sources: [782н](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/25045), [902н](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/25506), [903н](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/25496), [2022 amendment 279н](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/31327), [2025 amendment 287н](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/55545). Original PDF pages were rendered and visually read; scanned PDFs did not expose searchable text. Amendment items 11, 34 and 35 extend the three instruments to 1 September 2031; this does not extend an individual work permit.

### Height checklist closure

Compared 782н original PDF pages 2–23, clauses 1–92. Scope/avoidance/hierarchy; conditional routine-work exception; competence groups and roles; work/rescue plans; weather and falling objects; issuer/leader/producer responsibilities; re-entry after breaks; crew/hazard changes; closeout and register are covered by existing prompts plus the new cycle/access/rescue/restart/closeout checks. Clauses 64–74 distinguish a permit of at most 15 calendar days and one extension of at most 15 days from cancellation/reissue when specified circumstances change. Completed permits normally remain 30 days, while accident-related documents follow investigation records. No period, exception, competence group or weather acceptance is automatically granted by the application.

Later specialist equipment/rope/scaffold engineering clauses are not claimed exhaustively reviewed. Generic form fields link the applicable work plan and equipment checks; engineering calculations, actual rescue arrangements and specific exceptions remain outside this checklist's original scope.

### Confined-space checklist closure

Read original PDF pages 2–40, including rescue and signs. Clause 3 excludes hazardous production facilities governed by federal industrial-safety rules: new scope field records that distinction. Clauses 45–51 separate entry to assess the atmosphere from permission to perform the productive work. Clauses 67–79 establish shift-based permits, conditional longer validity, re-entry checks, cancellation, closeout and one-year completed-permit records (accident documents retained with investigation records). An extension is not the default. New fields record purpose, cycle and scope; existing/new measurement-competence, isolation, standby, rescue and restart checks cover the general workflow.

Measurement equipment and current results, outside observation, alarm/evacuation, communication, trained rescuers and rescue planning were checked against clauses 108–150 and 207–220. Do not copy an oxygen figure from hazard classification as a universal entry criterion. Hot-work atmosphere acceptance is distinct from entry acceptance. Clause 140 isolation exceptions need documented site assessment; the form does not approve them. Specialist cleaning, water/sewer, painting and welding methods referenced in later sections are not certified or prescribed by these generic prompts.

### Electrical terminology closure; technical task still open

Original 903н clause 5.8 (PDF pp.17–18), adjacent role clauses, and 279н item 14 (PDF p.5) establish the meaning of `допускающий`. This person prepares or assesses preparation of the workplace, briefs and admits the team, with appropriate electrical-safety group and appointment. It is distinct from permit issuer, responsible leader and work producer. `Группа по электробезопасности` means competence category, not crew size. The 2022 wording adjustment does not alter this meaning. Native terms stay Russian in all ten document languages. The new current-work check records appointment/authority/group/preparation/briefing without seeding names or qualifications.

Only original pp.11–12, 16–18 and amendment pp.3–6 were used for this batch. The rest of the de-energised-work procedure and relevant 2022 technical changes still require comparison. Therefore terminology closes but RU electrical source/task checkpoints remain open. RU hot likewise retains its current-consolidation and specialist-sector issues; the earlier MChS 1479 snapshot does not inherit 782н/902н permit periods.

## Québec hot work: code obtained, incorporated standard still open

Read [CNPI 2020 modified Québec](https://publications-cnrc.canada.ca/fra/voir/td/?id=645222bb-21e8-44cc-853e-178b7aeafbaa), section 5.2, and [RBQ explanation](https://www.rbq.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/medias/pdf/Publications/francais/cahier-explicatif-changements-cnpi-2020.pdf), introduction p.5. The adopted edition took effect 17 April 2025; the published transition permits prior provisions through 16 October 2026. Site applicability and later legal changes must still be checked rather than selecting the edition by UI locale.

Section 5.2.3.1 separates the default noncombustible work area from conditional precautions where that is impracticable. Under that route, surrounding/adjacent combustibles, continuous watch during and at least 60 minutes after work, and a separate final inspection matter. The final inspection can be four hours later or the specified more exhaustive inspection after the watch. No universal timer or clearance radius is inserted. New fields capture adopted edition/transition and separate watch/final inspection people and times. Full incorporated CSA welding standards are still unavailable to this review, so both topic checkpoints remain open.

## Saudi Arabia: fitness guide is not licensing implementation

The [June 2026 HRSD procedural guide](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2026-06/qrar-wzary-ba-tmad-aldlyl-alajrayy-llayht-fhwsat-allyaqt-almhnyt-walamrad-ghyr-alm-dyt.pdf) covers occupational fitness and confidential medical records. It does not resolve high-risk occupational-licensing implementation or sector PTW procedures. All four combinations retain those eight checkpoints. The added prompt records only reviewer/time of an authorised confirmation and explicitly excludes diagnoses, examination values and medical reports. It does not reproduce official medical forms, calculate fitness or set a periodic medical deadline. This is a data-minimisation instruction in a free-text field, not a claim that the software detects every sensitive entry.
