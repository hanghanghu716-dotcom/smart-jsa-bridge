Scope: Work Packages generic de-energised electrical checklist only. Checked 2026-10-06. No Case Study, guideline, database, account or production changes. Historical review reports describe their own snapshots; this report supersedes only the outstanding RU.electrical finding.

## Primary evidence and comparison

- [903н official publication](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/25496): original scan, PDF pp.11–56 (printed pp.10–55), chapters IV–XXIII. Chapters IV–VI were compared in the previous and current batches; the remaining general admission, interruption, closeout and technical-preparation chapters were visually read in this batch.
- [279н, 2022 amendment](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/31327): all 11 original PDF pages read. Reconcile amended text, not just the old scan. Items affecting specialist operations were identified but do not turn this checklist into an approved method for those operations.
- [287н, 2025 amendment](https://minjust.consultant.ru/documents/55545), item 35: extends 903н to 1 September 2031. Its reference to the 2022 amendment and the original amendment were compared. Extension of a regulation is not extension of a worker's permit.

## Terminology and data behavior

Seven new meaning groups are compared across Korean, English, German, Japanese, French, Italian, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese and Russian. Role names continue to be anchored by the existing Russian native-role bundle. “Work order” remains distinct from a permit; daily admission is not immigration/admission to a country; earthing is a protective electrical operation; reissue is not automatic renewal. No existing reviewed string is rewritten.

Six new labels, one notice and three reused checks add nine blank current-work fields only to RU.electrical. New work/copies clear these values even if legacy data marks them as standard. Existing saved documents retain their original fields, source snapshot and review version. Existing 597 terminology, 89 form and 64 task fingerprints must remain unchanged before the new closure is added.

This closes the two RU.electrical inventory checkpoints only for the declared generic checklist. It does not certify a statutory permit, approve actual switching, validate competence, provide live-work methods, calculate distances/arc protection, or replace equipment/sector-specific engineering. Source review is dated, not a promise that regulations never change.
